How it works

From observation to support, in four steps.

01

Observe

The teacher captures the moment within the classroom flow: “Deniz built a three-part pattern.” Ten seconds, one screen.

02

Understand

NOVA ECE maps the observation to the right domain; AI spots patterns, the teacher confirms.

03

Support

The teacher gets a planning suggestion; the family gets a clear summary and an at-home activity.

04

Follow the journey

Journey views and term reports; each child is compared only with their own past.

Trust & Science

We design trust as carefully as technology.

Separate consents

Distinct, revocable parental consent for photos, health tracking, AI use and publicity.

Role-based access

Everyone sees only what they should; a parent can never reach another child’s data.

Audit log

Who accessed what, and when — recorded and reviewable by school leadership.

Data minimisation

Only purpose-necessary data is collected, and deleted after its defined retention period.

Responsible AI

AI suggests, humans decide; every AI output is labelled and source-grounded.

Privacy by design

Designed with KVKK (Turkish data-protection) requirements in mind; consent management is built in.
