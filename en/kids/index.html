NOVA ECE Kids

Children who learn through stories and discover through play.

NOVA ECE Kids offers 100 interactive picture stories and 100 learning games for ages 3–6, in Turkish and English, with professional narration and word-by-word highlighted text.

Try it in your browserApp Store · Coming soonGoogle Play · Coming soon
A look inside

Every page is touchable, every game has a learning goal.

What’s inside?

A child-friendly world aligned with the early years curriculum.

Content is tagged with the learning areas of Türkiye’s Century Maarif Model for preschool: Language, Maths, Science, Social, Art, Music, Movement & Health.

100 interactive stories

Tap characters, make predictions, count together. Every story ends with a feelings and sequencing activity.

100 learning games

Counting, patterns, first sounds, colour mixing, rhythm, healthy choices… Each game has five short rounds.

Two languages, real voices

Studio narration in Turkish and English; each word lights up as it is read, building print awareness.

Teacher mode

Large text, hide-text and a classroom-screen layout. Learning outcomes and activity ideas for every story.

Grown-up corner

Two quick ideas to try together at home at the end of every game.

Ad-free and safe

No ads or chat. External links open only after an adult verification step.

New

Coding and STEAM: learning to think through play.

Two new apps have joined the NOVA ECE Kids family. Both run right in your browser; coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

NOVA ECE Code

Coding through play from preschool to Year 4: 4 worlds, 24 levels. Sequencing, loops, conditions and functions, plus a rhythm workshop, story stage and teacher panel.

Open Code

NOVA ECE STEAM Lab

23 narrated activities: predict and test (science), design and test (engineering), measure and count, sensors, robots and patterns. Teacher panel, learning goal mapping and at-home experiments.

Open STEAM Lab

For families and schools

Not screen time: time together.

NOVA ECE Kids is designed for short, calm, purposeful sessions. It asks for no account or personal details; progress stays on the device. Write to us for classroom use and school licensing.
